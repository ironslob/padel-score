How it works

From download to first point in three steps.

Install on your watch

Open Wrist Rally on Apple Watch, Garmin, or Wear OS. Start Match. Optional warm-up, then choose who serves. Defaults are best of three with Star Point — change them only if you need to.

Tap the side that won the rally

Big Us and Them targets. Games, sets, tie-breaks, and serve rotation are automatic. Wrong tap? Hit the same button again before the ring finishes.

Finish, then look back

Complete the match, end early, or discard a false start. On Apple Watch the iPhone companion holds history and notes. On Garmin, history stays on the watch.

Formats

Club night, box league, or FIP rules — same app.

Match length is a setting, not a project. Deuce format can change mid-match if the court next door is playing a different point.

Default

Star Point

Two advantage cycles, then the next rally decides. Used by FIP, Premier Padel, and the CUPRA FIP Tour in 2026.

One advantage

Silver Point

Play one advantage. If it is broken, the next point wins the game.

No-ad

Golden Point

40–40 is immediately decisive. No advantage phase at all.

Classic

Regular

Advantage repeats until one pair wins two points in a row.

Match length

One set, best of three, 2 sets + 10-point match tie-break, best of five, or continuous play for social hitting.

Tie-breaks included

Set tie-break at 6–6 (first to 7, win by 2). Super tie-break when you choose 2 sets + TB. Serve rotation and change-sides reminders are automatic.

FAQ

Straight answers before you walk on court.

Do I need my phone on court?

No. Scoring runs entirely on the watch. Apple Watch can sync history to iPhone afterwards. Garmin and Wear OS score standalone. Wear OS has no Android phone companion in this version.

Which watches are supported?

Apple Watch on current watchOS, Garmin watches via Connect IQ (Venu, Forerunner, vívoactive, fēnix / epix, Instinct, and other touch and button-only models), and Wear OS 4+ watches such as Pixel Watch and Galaxy Watch.

Does it support Star Point and golden point?

Yes. Star Point is the default. Silver point, golden point, and regular advantage are in Settings. You can change deuce format during a match; completed games keep their result.

What if I tap the wrong team?

Tap the same button again within about three seconds. The Actions screen can undo later in the match. Game and set summaries also offer undo before you continue.

Does Wrist Rally record a workout?

Yes. Starting a match tries to start a HealthKit workout, a Garmin FIT activity, or a Wear OS Health Services exercise. If another app already owns the session, you can continue without a workout or cancel and stop the other session first.

Is there an account or cloud sync?

Not in this version. Matches live on the device. No login, no backend, no live spectator sharing. That keeps scoring fast and private. Cloud sync is a later milestone.

Is Wrist Rally free?

Store listings will go live with the Wrist Rally name. Pricing will be shown on the App Store, Connect IQ, and Play Store when each build is published.
