Privacy Policy

Last updated 8 September 2026. Wrist Rally is built to keep score on your watch — not to build a profile of you.

What Wrist Rally is

Wrist Rally is a padel scoring app for Apple Watch (with an iPhone companion), Garmin Connect IQ watches, and Wear OS watches. This version is fully on-device. There is no Wrist Rally account, no Wrist Rally cloud, and no Wrist Rally backend that receives your matches.

Data we store on your device

To keep score and restore a match after a restart, the watch app stores locally:

  • The active match and completed match history (scores, events, timestamps, settings you chose)
  • Preferences such as deuce format, match length, labels, and warm-up

On iPhone, the companion app may also store match notes and a list of matches you deleted. Notes stay on the phone; they are not sent to the watch.

Health and fitness data

Starting a match tries to start a workout or activity so the session is tracked as training and, where the platform allows it, so Wrist Rally can return when you raise your wrist.

  • Apple Watch: HealthKit workout. Heart rate, calories, and similar metrics are stored by Apple Health, not by a Wrist Rally server.
  • Garmin: FIT activity recording. The file (including score fields we write) is handled by Garmin Connect according to Garmin’s policies.
  • Wear OS: Health Services exercise session on the watch. There is no Android phone companion in this version.

If another app already owns the workout session, Wrist Rally asks whether to continue without a workout or cancel.

Sync between Apple Watch and iPhone

When the paired iPhone is reachable, the watch can send the active match and history over Apple WatchConnectivity. That transfer happens on your devices. Wrist Rally does not operate a live-sharing service or spectator website in this version.

What we do not collect

Wrist Rally does not require an email address, login, or analytics SDK for scoring. We do not sell personal data. We do not run a match-sharing cloud in this version.

This website

wristrally.com is a static marketing site. It does not include an account system. If a future host adds standard access logs, those are the host’s logs, not match data from the app.

Changes

If the product adds cloud sync, accounts, or live sharing, this policy will be updated before those features collect data off-device. The revised policy will be posted on this page.
